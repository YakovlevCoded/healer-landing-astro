Bevita — Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-24

Effective: 2026-05-24

This Privacy Policy explains how Bevita Health AG ("Bevita", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you use the Bevita mobile app and related services.

We take your privacy seriously, especially because health data is among the most sensitive categories of personal information.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information you provide directly:

  • Account information: email address, optional name
  • Profile data: age, biological sex, weight, height, chronic conditions, medications, lifestyle factors
  • Onboarding quiz responses
  • Lab results you upload (photos, PDFs, manually entered values)
  • Symptom logs (location, type, intensity, duration)
  • Notes and journal entries
  • Subscription and payment information (processed by App Store / Google Play / Stripe — we do not store payment card details)

B. Information collected automatically:

  • Device information: device type, OS version, app version, language, timezone
  • Usage data: features used, screens viewed, session duration (anonymized via PostHog)
  • Crash reports (via Sentry)
  • Push notification tokens

C. Information we DO NOT collect:

  • Precise location (we use only timezone)
  • Contacts, photos library access (only specific photos you choose to upload)
  • Microphone, camera (except when you explicitly use the upload feature)
  • Browsing history outside the app
  • Social media accounts

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Provide the core service: extract data from your lab results, generate analyses, send personalized reminders
  • Improve the AI models (only with explicit consent — see Section 6)
  • Communicate with you about the service (password resets, important updates)
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions
  • Detect and prevent abuse, fraud, and security incidents
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do NOT use your data to:

  • Sell or share with advertisers
  • Build advertising profiles
  • Train third-party AI models without your consent
  • Provide insurance underwriting or pricing decisions
  • Share with employers or healthcare payers

3. Legal Basis (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation, we process your data based on:

  • **Contract performance** (Article 6(1)(b)): to provide the service you subscribed to
  • **Legitimate interest** (Article 6(1)(f)): for fraud prevention, service improvement
  • **Consent** (Article 6(1)(a) + Article 9(2)(a) for health data): for AI processing of your lab results and analysis
  • **Legal obligation** (Article 6(1)(c)): for tax, regulatory compliance

For health-related data (Article 9 special categories), we rely on explicit consent which you provide during onboarding. You may withdraw consent at any time.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Processors

We share data only with these third-party processors, each under a Data Processing Agreement (DPA):

| Processor | Purpose | Location |

|---|---|---|

| Supabase (Postgres + storage) | Primary database and file storage | EU (Frankfurt) |

| OpenRouter (openrouter.ai) | Routing gateway for the AI providers below | USA |

| Google Gemini (via OpenRouter) | Extraction and analysis of biomarkers and lab document content | USA |

| DeepSeek (via OpenRouter) | AI-generated narrative summaries and in-app chat answers | Routed through OpenRouter (USA) |

| RevenueCat | Subscription management (inactive in v1.0) | USA |

| Apple App Store / Google Play | Payment processing | USA |

| Expo Push Notifications | Push notification delivery | USA |

| PostHog | Anonymized usage analytics | EU (self-hosted) |

| Sentry | Crash reporting | USA |

What is sent to AI providers: only the text and values extracted from documents you upload (biomarkers, units, ranges, dates), your age and sex, symptoms or notes you log, and messages you write in the in-app AI chat. Your name, email, account identifiers, and uploaded raw files are not sent to AI providers.

Cross-border data transfers: Some of our processors are outside the EU/EEA. For transfers to the USA, we rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and on your explicit consent (collected at onboarding via the AI Data Sharing consent screen), plus Standard Contractual Clauses where available.

You may withdraw your AI consent any time from Profile → Legal & Privacy. Doing so disables AI features (lab extraction, narrative analysis, chat).

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security:

  • Transport encryption (TLS 1.3) for all data transmission
  • Encryption at rest for biomarker values (libsodium with per-user keys)
  • Row-level security in Postgres
  • Signed URLs for file storage (5-minute expiry)
  • Multi-factor authentication available for accounts
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing (post-launch)
  • Audit logs of all data access

In case of a data breach affecting your data, we will notify you within 72 hours as required by GDPR Article 33.

6. AI Processing of Your Data

When you upload a lab result or use the in-app chat, the relevant content is:

1. Stored in our EU-based storage (Supabase, file uploads) and database (extracted values).

2. Sent through OpenRouter (openrouter.ai, USA) to Google Gemini for document extraction and to DeepSeek for narrative analysis and chat answers.

3. Returned as structured biomarkers and plain-language explanations.

4. Stored in our database (encrypted at rest) so you can see history and trends.

OpenRouter, Google Gemini, and DeepSeek do NOT train models on your data per their stated terms (verified at signup). Transient processing in their infrastructure is required to perform the extraction and chat.

If you withdraw consent for AI processing, we will:

  • Stop processing new uploads with AI
  • Delete previously extracted biomarker data (you can request)
  • Continue providing manual entry features without AI

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for the following periods:

  • Account and profile: while your account is active + 30 days after deletion
  • Lab results and analyses: while your account is active + 30 days
  • Anonymous usage analytics: 26 months (PostHog default)
  • Crash logs: 90 days
  • Audit logs: 2 years (legal requirement in some jurisdictions)
  • Payment records: 7 years (tax/accounting requirement)

After account deletion, we anonymize remaining data within 30 days. Aggregated, anonymized statistics may be retained indefinitely.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the following rights:

Under GDPR (EU/EEA users):

  • Access your data (Article 15)
  • Correct inaccurate data (Article 16)
  • Delete your data ("right to be forgotten") (Article 17)
  • Restrict processing (Article 18)
  • Data portability — export your data (Article 20)
  • Object to processing (Article 21)
  • Withdraw consent at any time (Article 7(3))
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

Under CCPA (California, USA users):

  • Know what data we collect
  • Delete your data
  • Opt out of "sale" of data (we don't sell, but you can object)
  • Non-discrimination for exercising rights

Russian users:

  • Rights under Federal Law No. 152-FZ on Personal Data, where applicable

To exercise any right: email privacy@healer.app or use the in-app data export and deletion tools (Settings → Privacy).

We respond within 30 days (GDPR) or 45 days (CCPA).

9. Children's Privacy

Bevita is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us at privacy@healer.app and we will delete the data.

For users between 13-18 in jurisdictions where teens can consent (varies), parental consent is required.

10. Sharing with Healthcare Providers

You can choose to share data with a healthcare provider via:

  • PDF export (you control the file)
  • Future doctor portal (Phase 3, opt-in only)

We do not share your data with any healthcare provider without your explicit action.

11. Account Deletion

To delete your account:

1. Settings → Account → Delete Account

2. Confirm via email link (sent to verify your identity)

3. All data is queued for deletion within 30 days

4. Anonymized analytics may be retained as described in Section 7

You can also email privacy@healer.app to request deletion.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be communicated via:

  • In-app notification on next launch
  • Email to your registered address
  • Push notification

You will be asked to re-accept the policy if material changes affect your rights.
