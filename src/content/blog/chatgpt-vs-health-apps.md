Everyone's asking ChatGPT about their blood test results. And it's genuinely useful — you paste in your numbers, it explains what they mean. But there's a fundamental problem: **ChatGPT forgets everything the moment you close the tab.**

## What ChatGPT does well

- Explains individual lab values in plain language
- Answers "is my ferritin of 32 normal?"
- Suggests follow-up questions for your doctor
- Available instantly, no setup required

## What ChatGPT can't do

- **Remember your last result.** Ask it about your ferritin next month and it has no idea what it was before.
- **Track trends.** "Has my cholesterol been going up?" — ChatGPT can't answer that because it doesn't have your history.
- **Connect symptoms to labs.** You logged fatigue 4 times this week. Your ferritin dropped from 85 to 32 over 18 months. ChatGPT doesn't see that pattern.
- **Remind you.** "Your last full panel was 9 months ago — time to retest." No cron jobs, no push notifications.
- **Export a PDF.** Your doctor can't read a ChatGPT conversation. They need a clinical document.
- **Guarantee privacy.** Your health data goes to OpenAI's servers. With a dedicated health app, your data stays in your account.

## The three levels of AI health tools

Think of it as a spectrum:

**Level 1: Chatbot** (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini)
- You ask, it answers
- No memory between sessions
- No data storage
- No integration with your actual records

**Level 2: AI wrapper** (many "AI health" apps)
- Slap a chatbot on top of your data
- Some memory, but mostly generic advice
- Limited export, no doctor sharing
- "Ask our AI" as the main feature

**Level 3: Health record with AI** (Bevita)
- Structured data model (45+ biomarkers, canonical keys)
- Vector search across your entire history
- RAG-powered synthesis (connects dots over time)
- Export, sharing, reminders built in
- AI is a tool, not the product

## Which one do you need?

**If you have one question about one lab result:** ChatGPT is fine. Free, fast, accurate enough.

**If you want to understand your health over time:** You need a proper health record. The AI should enhance the record, not replace it.

The irony is that ChatGPT itself will tell you: "I recommend tracking these values over time and retesting in 3-6 months." It knows it can't do that. It's suggesting you use a tool that can.
