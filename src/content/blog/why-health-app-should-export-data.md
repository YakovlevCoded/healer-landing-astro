You've been tracking your health for months. You've uploaded 12 lab results, logged 47 symptoms, and connected your wearable. Your health data lives in the app.

Then your doctor says: "Can you send me your recent results?"

And you realize... you can't.

## The export problem

Most health apps treat your data like it belongs to them. You can view it, share screenshots, maybe copy a single value. But try to export a comprehensive summary? Not happening.

This is one of the most common complaints we hear:

> "I've been using [health app] for 18 months. When I went to a new doctor, I had to manually type out every result from memory. The app was useless."

It's not just convenience — it's a **clinical problem**. Your doctor needs:
- Your complete lab history (not just the latest)
- Trend charts showing changes over time
- Context about symptoms you've been tracking
- Your medication list and chronic conditions

Without this, your doctor is flying blind.

## What a good export looks like

A proper health data export should be:

### 1. Chronological
All your studies organized by date. Your doctor should be able to flip through your health history like a timeline.

### 2. Visual
Trend charts for key biomarkers. "Your ferritin dropped from 85 to 32 over 18 months" — that's a trend your doctor needs to see, not a table of numbers.

### 3. AI-curated
A summary that connects the dots: "Based on your last 3 panels, these are the patterns worth discussing with your doctor." Not just raw data — actionable insights.

### 4. Shareable
A clean PDF or a shareable link that your doctor can open without downloading an app. Your doctor shouldn't need to create an account to see your health data.

### 5. Multi-language
If you see a Russian-speaking doctor but your app is in English, the export should handle both. Health data doesn't respect language barriers.

## What to look for in a health app

When choosing a health tracking app, ask these questions:

1. **Can I export my data as PDF?** If the answer is no, walk away.
2. **Can I generate a shareable link?** Your doctor shouldn't need to install an app.
3. **Does it show trends over time?** A single lab result is a snapshot. Trends are the story.
4. **Does it connect symptoms to labs?** This is where the real insights come from.
5. **Is my data actually mine?** Check the privacy policy. Can you delete everything?
