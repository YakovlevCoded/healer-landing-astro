You've been tracking your health for months. You've uploaded 12 lab results, logged 47 symptoms, connected your wearable.

Then your doctor says: "Can you send me your recent results?"

And you realize... you can't.

## The export problem

Most health apps treat your data like it belongs to them. You can view it, share screenshots, maybe copy a single value. But try to export a comprehensive summary? Not happening.

It's not just convenience — it's a **clinical problem**. Your doctor needs your complete lab history, trend charts, symptom context, and medication list. Without it, they're flying blind.

## What a good export looks like

1. **All your studies in one place**, organized by date
2. **Trend charts** showing changes over time
3. **AI-curated summary** connecting the dots
4. **A shareable link** your doctor can open without downloading an app
5. **Works in any language** — health data doesn't respect language barriers
