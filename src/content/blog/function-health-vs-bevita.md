Function Health and Bevita get mentioned in the same breath, but they solve different halves of the problem. One is about **getting tested**; the other is about **understanding the tests you get**. Knowing which gap you're trying to fill makes the choice obvious.

## The stat most people don't know

Most adults already get blood drawn more often than they realize — annual physicals, pre-op panels, pregnancy, chronic-condition monitoring — but the results scatter across portals and PDFs and are never looked at as a trend. So for a lot of people the missing piece isn't *more* testing; it's actually reading the testing they already do. That distinction is the whole comparison.

## What Function Health is

Function Health is a **paid annual membership** that orders a large panel — 100+ biomarkers — through partner labs, then shows you the results in a dashboard with clinician review. Its pitch is breadth: one big draw covering far more markers than a typical checkup.

- **Strength:** comprehensive testing you might not otherwise get ordered.
- **Cost:** an annual fee, on top of any testing you already receive through insurance or your doctor.
- **Model:** it's a testing service first, with a results interface attached.

## What Bevita is

Bevita is an **app that reads the labs you already have.** You upload a PDF from your doctor, hospital, or any lab, and it interprets and tracks them.

- **Strength:** turns existing, scattered results into a readable trend — plus calculated markers and symptom connections.
- **Cost:** free to start; no test-kit fees because it doesn't sell tests.
- **Model:** interpretation first — it's lab-agnostic and works with whatever bloodwork you get, including a Function panel.

## Where they overlap and where they don't

- **Getting a broad panel ordered:** Function does this; Bevita doesn't order tests.
- **Reading results over time:** both show trends, but Bevita focuses on derived markers (like HOMA-IR for [insulin resistance](/blog/how-to-know-if-you-have-insulin-resistance/)), optimal-vs-normal flags, and tying labs to symptoms like [fatigue](/blog/always-tired-blood-markers/) or [brain fog](/blog/brain-fog-blood-markers/).
- **Cost structure:** Function is a recurring membership; Bevita is free to start and works with tests you already pay for elsewhere.

## The bottom line

- **Choose Function Health** if you want a broad panel ordered for you and the annual fee fits your budget.
- **Choose Bevita** if you already get bloodwork and want it actually read, tracked, and connected to how you feel — for free, from any lab.
